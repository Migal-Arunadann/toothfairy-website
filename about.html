ABOUT THE PRACTICE

A clinical standard built around patience, clarity, and comfort.

Located on Paper Mills Road in Perambur, Tooth Fairy Dental Clinic was established with a straightforward conviction: dental care is most effective when patients feel completely heard, fully informed, and unhurried throughout their treatment.

OUR PRACTICE PHILOSOPHY

Clinical competence grounded in consideration.

Many patients hesitate to visit a dental clinic because of past experiences with rushed appointments, clinical jargon, or unmanaged discomfort. At Tooth Fairy Dental Clinic, our priority is changing that expectation.

We believe that a successful clinical outcome begins long before an instrument is lifted—it begins by thoroughly explaining what will happen, why it is necessary, and checking in on your comfort at each stage of care.

"Every procedure, from tooth alignment to complex surgery, is explained step-by-step so our patients stay in control of their dental care."

— Dr. Tamanna, Lead Dentist
LEAD DENTIST

Dr. Tamanna

B.D.S., F.A.G.E.

Dedicated Dental Consultations in Perambur

As the lead clinician at Tooth Fairy Dental Clinic, Dr. Tamanna holds the qualifications of B.D.S. (Bachelor of Dental Surgery) and F.A.G.E. (Fellow of Academy of General Education), as prominently indicated on the clinic signboard.

Dr. Tamanna's clinical consultations focus on conservative dental preservation: identifying the root cause of tooth pain, saving natural tooth structure through careful root canal therapy whenever viable, guiding patients through orthodontic alignment, and managing impacted wisdom teeth with minimal patient discomfort.

Rather than generic treatment packages, consultations with Dr. Tamanna are dedicated one-on-one sessions where questions are welcomed and transparent guidance is provided.

CLINIC CHARACTERISTICS

What defines our practice environment.

Concrete clinical practices that create trust and ease for patients across Chennai.

Unhurried Time

We avoid overbooking. Each patient is allocated sufficient time so Dr. Tamanna can listen attentively to symptoms, answer queries, and carry out procedures without haste.

Clear Explanations

Patients appreciate knowing what is happening. We explain diagnostic findings in simple, transparent terms and outline the steps before starting treatment.

Welcoming & Inclusive

Tooth Fairy Dental Clinic proudly identifies as women-owned and provides an LGBTQ+ friendly, respectful, and dignified healthcare setting for all patients and families.
